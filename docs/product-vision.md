# Product vision

## Mission

Export HQ is the operating system for international growth: software, reliable data, managed workflows, accountable experts, partner execution, and evidence-aware AI in one workspace.

The first commercial wedge is Bangladesh to Germany and the EU, while every core primitive remains origin-, destination-, currency-, language-, and sector-neutral.

## Product promise

The customer should always understand:

1. how its export business is performing;
2. what needs attention now;
3. who owns each next action; and
4. what Export HQ is actively doing on its behalf.

The platform is the system of record. Managed service specialists work on the same products, requirements, evidence, documents, and tasks the customer sees. Data remains portable and customer-owned.

## Phase 1 outcome

An exporter can create an organization, describe the company and a product, select a target market, receive an evidence-shaped readiness plan, upload documents, and track both customer-owned and Export HQ-owned work. An authorized operator reviews the same customer context without becoming a hidden customer member.

## Product principles

- Action, ownership, deadline, and next step precede charts.
- Compliance guidance carries sources, effective dates, confidence, and review state.
- AI suggestions never masquerade as legal certainty or silently change business records.
- Complexity is revealed progressively; mobile prioritizes status, approvals, tasks, messages, and uploads.
- Retention comes from accumulated value and institutional memory, not artificial lock-in.
