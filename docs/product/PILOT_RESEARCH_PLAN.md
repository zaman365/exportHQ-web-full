# Pilot research plan

External real data remains prohibited until R1 exits.

The future invitation-only cohort should include established and first/second-shipment Bangladesh exporters, at least two goods sectors, and EU/UK lanes. Capture time to first value, canonical-field reuse, upload/scan/review outcomes, task ownership, support minutes, extraction corrections, trust, willingness to pay, replaced spreadsheet/email/WhatsApp burden and realized-proceeds outcomes.

Every participant requires an approved agreement, named support owner, service boundary, data handling statement and exit/deletion path. Pilot observations may change pricing and scope; they do not weaken tenant, evidence or approval controls.
