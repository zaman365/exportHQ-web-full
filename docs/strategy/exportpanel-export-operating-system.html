Core record

Everything connects through an Export Lane.

One business + one controlled product and HS code + one destination + one buyer segment + one commercial route. Every signal, task, document, decision, provider, quotation, shipment, and payment must point back to this record.

Export Studio is the transaction and coordination center.

The Studio should present lane health, economics, deal milestones, buyer trust, provider paths, finance readiness, shipment control, policy exposure and collaboration in one coherent surface. Contextual hint icons must explain every non-obvious metric and link to the managed Learning Center collection.

Delivery sequence

Build the commercial spine before integrations.

  1. Foundation: production auth/data, Export Lane model, entitlement rules and audit boundaries.
  2. First revenue: economics, paid readiness reports, provider requests and managed readiness sprints.
  3. Retention: buyer trust, quotation/deal room, shipment documents and proceeds tracking.
  4. Network: finance/logistics/lab integrations, SME clusters and institution dashboards.
  5. Moat: anonymized cycle-time, rejection, margin and lane-performance benchmarks.

Commercial trust guardrails

Revenue must never weaken exporter outcomes.

  • Provider quality ranking is independent of sponsorship; commercial placement is visibly labelled.
  • Referral commissions are disclosed before a request is shared.
  • Scores expose their inputs, confidence, evidence period and review date.
  • Legal, customs, financial and product-safety conclusions remain controlled by authorities or qualified professionals.
  • Public previews never render private organization, document, buyer or payment data.
  • Every mutation rechecks organization, role, entitlement and resource ownership on the server.
