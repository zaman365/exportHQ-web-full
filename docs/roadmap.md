# Roadmap

## Phase 0 — foundation

Monorepo, design system, authentication boundary, organization tenancy, RBAC, PostgreSQL schema/migrations, audit framework, customer and ops shells, strict TypeScript, CI-ready quality commands, and isolation tests.

## Phase 1 — export core

Company/facility onboarding, products, target markets, readiness requirements, evidence documents, tasks, account team, messages, notifications, customer dashboard, and internal review.

The first slice in this repository visualizes and models that loop. Production persistence, upload signing/scanning, and live Clerk webhooks are the next activation work described in the implementation status.

## Phase 2 — export growth

Markets, export-specific buyer CRM, opportunities, outreach history, RFQs, samples, quotations, and growth planning.

## Phase 3 — trade operations

Orders, export-relevant production milestones, inspections, quality, freight, shipments, customs, invoices, payments, and claims.

## Phase 4 — intelligence

Evidence-backed AI, document extraction, requirement refresh, risk detection, recommendations, evaluations, and advanced analytics.

## Phase 5 — network

Scoped buyer and partner portals, partner work orders, financing/insurance referrals, and APIs where commercial traction warrants them.
