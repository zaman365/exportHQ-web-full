# 01 · ExportPanel product brand

## What it is

ExportPanel is the **system of record for a company's export operation** and the
shared surface where the customer's team, Export HQ specialists and approved
third parties do work that is visible to everyone with access.

It is not a dashboard. A dashboard reports; ExportPanel is where the work
happens and where accountability is recorded.

## The product promise, restated

The parent brand promises four answers. ExportPanel is where they are given:

| Question | Where it is answered |
| --- | --- |
| How is my export business performing? | Export health score, readiness by area, metric strip |
| What needs attention now? | Action centre, ownership tabs, requirement cards |
| Who owns each next action? | Owner on every row; the three-way ownership tabs |
| What is Export HQ doing for me? | The managed work column, the specialist team panel |

Those four blocks are the spine of the overview. Anything added must not push
them below the fold.

## Register

The website persuades. ExportPanel operates. Same brand, lower volume.

| | Website | ExportPanel |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Job** | Convince and qualify | Operate and account |
| **Loudest element** | The headline | The customer's data |
| **Type** | Display grotesque at 40–74px | UI grotesque at 13–30px |
| **Motion** | Ambient, scroll-linked | Only on state change |
| **Colour** | Signal used to lead the eye | Signal reserved for the primary action and active nav |
| **Density** | Generous | Compact |
| **Decoration** | Aurora, grain, cartography | None |
| **Emotion** | Ambition | Control |

**The register test:** take a screenshot of an ExportPanel screen and a website
section, desaturate both, and place them side by side. They should look like the
same company and *not* like the same page.

## Naming inside the product

- The product is **ExportPanel**. It appears in the browser title and in
  cross-links from the website.
- The chrome says **Export HQ** — the lockup is the company's, because the
  accountability signal is "Export HQ is doing this work".
- The customer's own organisation name is the largest identity on screen, in the
  organisation switcher and the workspace header.

That ordering is deliberate: **product name in the title bar, company name in the
chrome, customer name in the content.**

## Tone in-product

Neutral and factual. The product does not sell.

| Do | Do not |
| --- | --- |
| "What happens next" | "Your export journey awaits!" |
| "Waiting for you · Due 15 Aug" | "Action needed ASAP" |
| "4 of 7 requirements completed" | "Almost there!" |
| "Suggested — needs specialist review" | "Our AI recommends" |

The only warm moment is the greeting ("Good morning, Nadia.") and the named
specialists. Warmth comes from people, not from the interface talking.

## What ExportPanel must never look like

- A consumer app: no illustrations, no confetti, no mascots, no gamification of
  compliance.
- A generic admin template: no purple-on-white SaaS defaults, no icon-only rails
  without labels, no chart wall on the landing screen.
- A white-labelled portal: a customer must never be unable to tell that Export HQ
  is the system doing the work.
